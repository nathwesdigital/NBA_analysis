Customer Insight Atlas

A pure data breakdown of what is inside the customer file.

This dashboard is exploratory rather than prescriptive. It surfaces where customers sit across lifecycle, value, channel, household type, shopping categories, and linked-brand coverage so you can inspect the shape of the dataset before deciding what to do with it.

Reading guide

"Blended value" uses TOTAL_SPEND_365D first, then falls back to TOTAL_YEARLY_SPEND, then MONETARY when current spend is not populated.

The cluster explorer below is interactive. Click any cluster to inspect its value profile, channel mix, lifestages, family composition, and top shopping categories.
